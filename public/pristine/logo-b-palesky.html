Type discipline across every mark: letters are always Inter Light caps at the wordmark's +0.26em tracking, descriptors Inter Medium — one face, one voice. Nothing on this board is saved into the project — everything lives in session scratch until a direction is approved. All four Intracoastal colors are in play: green as the ink, shell as the ground, coral as the single accent per surface, and Haint Blue doing what the palette built it for — the large quiet field.
