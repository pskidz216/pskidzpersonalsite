Where we work

Start with where you live.

Every street here asks a different question. Ponte Vedra has salt, Ortega has a review board, Riverside has shade. Pick the place and the answer is already written.

Ponte Vedra Beach

Oceanfront exposure. Salt and UV halve the life of a standard system, so this is marine-grade work or a repaint in four years.

Atlantic Beach

Painted cottages three blocks off the water. Cedar and fiber cement sit on the same street and need different prep.

Ortega

Tudor and Mediterranean Revival, 1920s stucco over hollow tile. Anything street-facing goes to the review board.

Avondale

Craftsman bungalows on original lath and plaster. The front elevation follows historic-district rules, the back does not.

Avondale. Historic restoration.

Original lath and plaster moves. Filling a crack without keying into sound plaster buys one dry season. A bonding skim, then a flat finish that will not telegraph the repair.

How it runs

Four steps, and the second one is the whole job.

01 Consultation

A detailed walkthrough and an honest conversation about scope, surfaces and process. We record video of the walkthrough, so nothing gets remembered wrong later.

02 Preparation

Clean, repair, sand, prime, protect. Preparation is where premium results are earned, and where shortcuts create the failures that show up weeks later.

03 Application

Products, methods and sequence exactly as scoped. Drying and curing times are respected between coats, not compressed to finish early.

04 Final walkthrough

We walk every painted surface with you against the original scope. Touch-ups are addressed immediately, on site.
