Financing

Paint now, pay over time.

Exteriors on the First Coast are five-figure work, and waiting usually makes them more expensive, not less. Financing is available on larger projects through a third-party lender: you apply in minutes, the decision is fast, and the terms come from them in writing before you commit to anything.

How it works.

01

Get the written estimate first. The price is the price, financed or not — there is no financed markup.

02

Apply with the lender in minutes, from your phone. Decisions usually come back fast.

03

Approved terms in writing, then the work is scheduled like any other job, walkthrough and all.

Worth knowing

  • Rates, terms and approval come from the lender, not from us
  • No deposit to get the estimate, ever
  • Any payment schedule is written into the estimate you sign
  • Military and first responder discounts apply before financing math

Straight answers

  • We are not a lender and do not give financial advice
  • Financing is optional; cash and check price the same
  • If the numbers do not work for you, we will say so and phase the job instead
