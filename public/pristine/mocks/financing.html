Financing

Paint now, pay over time.

Larger painting and restoration projects can be a significant investment. Financing gives you the option to complete the work now and spread the cost over time. You’ll review your options and terms directly with the lender before making a decision.

How it works.

01

Get your written estimate first. Your scope and project price are established before financing enters the conversation.

02

Apply directly with the lender. They handle the approval process and provide your financing options and terms.

03

Once financing is in place, we schedule your project and move forward like any other Pristine Coastal job.

Worth knowing

  • Approval, rates and terms are determined by the lender, not Pristine Coastal.
  • Your estimate is free and carries no obligation.
  • Your project scope, price and payment requirements are clearly outlined before work begins.
  • Military and first responder discounts are applied before financing.

Straight answers

  • Financing is provided through a third-party lender. Approval, rates and terms are determined by the lender.
  • Financing is optional. Your Pristine Coastal project price remains the same regardless of how you choose to pay.
  • If financing isn’t the right fit, we can discuss phasing the project into manageable stages when the scope allows.

Start with the project.

Start with a free estimate and a clearly defined scope. Once you know what your project requires and what it will cost, you can decide whether financing makes sense for you.
