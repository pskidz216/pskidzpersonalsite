Local knowledge

What the ocean does to paint here.

Salt, humidity, UV

Three blocks from the ocean is a different climate than three miles inland. Salt air works into every seam, humidity keeps surfaces from ever fully drying, and the sun bleaches south and east elevations first. Prep is most of the job: full wash, bonding primer, and an extra coat where the wind hits.

Older cottages

The original beach cottages have wood siding and decades of paint history. We check for soft wood and moisture before quoting, because painting over a damp board buys you two good years and one bad one. Where the wood is gone, we replace it before any color goes on.

Newer builds and condos

Stucco and fiber-cement hold up better, but builder-grade paint on the ocean side still starts chalking around year four. A marine-grade repaint over full prep typically runs eight to ten years. For condos and multi-unit buildings we work floor by floor and keep walkways open.

Rentals

For rental and multi-unit owners we schedule around turnovers, photograph every unit before and after, and keep a written scope so the same finish shows up in every repaint.

Jacksonville Beach questions, answered.

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in Jacksonville Beach?−

Most Jacksonville Beach exteriors run $7,000 to $22,000 depending on size, substrate and how close the home sits to the ocean. Oceanfront homes sit at the top of the range because the system is marine-grade and the prep is heavier. Every range is published on our pricing page, not gated behind a form.

How often should a beach home be repainted?+

Builder-grade paint on the ocean side often needs attention by year four. A marine-grade system over full prep typically runs eight to ten years, and an annual rinse of the salt extends it.

My drywall feels soft near the windows. Is that the humidity?+

Usually it is moisture getting in around the opening, not the air itself. We find the entry point and fix it first, then replace the damaged board. Painting over soft drywall hides the problem for a season and makes the repair bigger later.

Can you work around a rental schedule?+

Yes. We schedule between turnovers, contain and clean daily, and photograph before and after so you can see the condition of every unit without driving out.

Can you match the existing color after a repair or addition?+

Yes. We pull the match from a protected surface rather than a faded one, and we spray out a sample on the wall before committing, because coastal fading means the can color and the wall color are rarely the same.

Do you handle storm and water damage?+

Yes. Restoration is half our work: drywall, trim and repaint after a leak or a storm, documented so your insurance has what it needs.
