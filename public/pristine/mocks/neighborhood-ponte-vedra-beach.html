Local knowledge

What the ocean does to paint here.

Salt and UV

Oceanfront exposure halves the life of a standard system. The windward elevation is a different job from the same house four miles inland: full wash, bonding primer, and an extra coat where the wind hits.

The four-year repaint

Builder-grade paint on the ocean side starts chalking in year three. A marine-grade system costs more once and is the cheaper decision by year six.

Sawgrass and Marsh Landing

Both communities review exterior colors and sheen. We prepare the submission with you, and the scheme gets approved before a drop cloth goes down.

Intracoastal and Palm Valley

Homes on the marsh side trade salt spray for shade and moisture. Mildew-resistant coatings and a harder look at north elevations, where the sun never quite finishes the drying.

Ponte Vedra questions, answered.

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in Ponte Vedra Beach?−

Most Ponte Vedra exteriors run $9,000 to $28,000 depending on size, substrate and exposure. Oceanfront homes sit at the top of the range because the system is marine-grade and the prep is heavier. Every range is published on our pricing page, not gated behind a form.

How often should an oceanfront home be repainted?+

Builder-grade paint on the windward side often needs attention by year four. A marine-grade system applied over full prep typically runs eight to ten years, and an annual rinse of the salt extends it.

Do you handle the Sawgrass and Marsh Landing approval process?+

Yes. We prepare the color and sheen submission with you, and the scheme is approved by the review board before any work is scheduled.

Can you work while we are living in the house?+

Yes, most of our interior work is in occupied homes. Rooms are contained, cleaned and reset every day, and the schedule is agreed before we start.

Can you match the existing color after a repair or addition?+

Yes. We pull the match from a protected surface rather than a faded one, and we spray out a sample on the wall before committing, because coastal fading means the can color and the wall color are rarely the same.

Do you handle storm and water damage?+

Yes. Restoration is half our work: drywall, trim and repaint after a leak or a storm, documented so your insurance has what it needs.
