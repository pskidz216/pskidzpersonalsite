Ponte Vedra questions, answered.

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in Ponte Vedra Beach?−

Most Ponte Vedra exteriors run $9,000 to $28,000 depending on size, substrate and exposure. Oceanfront homes sit at the top of the range because the system is marine-grade and the prep is heavier. Every range is published on our pricing page, not gated behind a form.

How often should an oceanfront home be repainted?+

Builder-grade paint on the windward side often needs attention by year four. A marine-grade system applied over full prep typically runs eight to ten years, and an annual rinse of the salt extends it.

Do you handle the Sawgrass and Marsh Landing approval process?+

Yes. We prepare the color and sheen submission with you, and the scheme is approved by the review board before any work is scheduled.

Can you work while we are living in the house?+

Yes, most of our interior work is in occupied homes. Rooms are contained, cleaned and reset every day, and the schedule is agreed before we start.
