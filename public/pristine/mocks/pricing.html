What Affects the Price?

Surface Condition

The condition of your home determines how much preparation is needed before painting. Cracks, failing caulk, damaged drywall, and other repairs can increase the scope and cost of the project.

Coastal Exposure

Homes on the First Coast are exposed to salt, moisture, intense sun, and coastal weather. These conditions can affect the preparation and coating system we recommend for longer-lasting protection.

Level of Finish

New paint will refresh the color and finish, but it will not hide existing imperfections. If you want a smoother, more uniform appearance, additional preparation can be included to correct surface flaws before painting.

Common Pricing Questions

How much does it cost to paint a 3,000 sq ft house interior in Jacksonville?−

A 3,000 sq. ft. interior typically ranges from $9,000–$13,000, depending on the surfaces being painted, their condition, and the level of preparation needed. More extensive surface correction or specialty finishes can increase the cost. Your scheduled estimate will provide an exact price based on your home and project scope.

What is included in the estimate?+

Your written estimate clearly outlines the project scope, preparation, repairs, materials, and cleanup. Any exclusions will also be clearly identified so you know exactly what is—and is not—included before the project begins.

Is the estimate free?+

Yes. Your estimate is free and carries no obligation. We’ll take the time to understand your project, evaluate the surfaces, and clearly define the scope before providing your price.

Do you offer military discounts?+

Yes. Pristine Coastal is veteran-owned, and we’re proud to offer military discounts on residential projects. Let us know about your service when scheduling your estimate.
