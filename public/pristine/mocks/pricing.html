Three things move the number.

Surface condition

Prep is most of the job. Chalking stucco, failing caulk or water-stained drywall get corrected before paint, and that labor is where ranges widen.

Exposure

Oceanfront and Intracoastal homes need marine-grade systems. The material costs more once, and lasts roughly twice as long as builder grade on the windward side.

Finish tier

Standard, premium or fine finish. Fine finish means sprayed doors and millwork, hand-rubbed, with a Level 5 wall where light rakes across it.

Why the number is not behind a form.

Gating a range loses most of the people who came to find it. So the range is free. The written breakdown is what the walkthrough produces: the assumptions, what is and is not included, the prep standard, and the questions worth asking any contractor before you sign.
