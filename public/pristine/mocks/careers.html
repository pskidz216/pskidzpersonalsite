Join the crew.

We hire people who show up on time and care whether the line is straight, and we teach the rest. If you paint like it matters — or want to learn to — introduce yourself. Every job here ends on camera, which means your work gets seen.

What we look for

  • On time, every time — the schedule is the promise
  • Care over speed; speed comes with reps
  • Comfortable being held to a filmed standard
  • Veterans: your discipline translates here, directly

What you get

  • Steady First Coast work, from the Beaches to St. Augustine
  • One crew, one lead — no being shuffled between jobs mid-week
  • A standard you can point at: the walkthrough is your portfolio
  • Pay and openings listed when posted
