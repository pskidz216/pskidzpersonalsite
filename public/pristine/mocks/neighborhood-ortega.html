Ortega questions, answered.

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in Ortega?−

Most Ortega exteriors run $14,000 to $38,000; estate-scale riverfront homes can run higher, driven by elevation length, staging and prep. Every range is published on our pricing page, not gated behind a form.

How long does a full exterior take?+

Most single-family exteriors run one to two weeks; large or estate-scale homes run two to four depending on prep and weather. You get the schedule in writing at the walkthrough and a call if weather moves it.

My drywall feels soft near the windows. Is that the humidity?+

Usually it is moisture getting in around the opening, not the air itself. We find the entry point and fix it first, then replace the damaged board. Painting over soft drywall hides the problem for a season and makes the repair bigger later.

Can you match the existing color after a repair or addition?+

Yes. We pull the match from a protected surface rather than a faded one, and we spray out a sample on the wall before committing, because fading means the can color and the wall color are rarely the same.

Can you work while we are living in the house?+

Yes, most of our interior work is in occupied homes. Rooms are contained, cleaned and reset every day, and the schedule is agreed before we start.

Do you handle storm and water damage?+

Yes. Restoration is half our work: drywall, trim and repaint after a leak or a storm, documented so your insurance has what it needs.
