The guarantee

If our work fails, we fix it. Free.

Every job is covered by a written workmanship guarantee: if the finish peels, blisters or lets go because of how we prepped or applied it, we come back and make it right at no cost. It is in the estimate you sign, not in fine print you find later.

What the guarantee covers

  • Peeling, blistering or adhesion failure caused by our prep or application
  • Finish defects: lap marks, holidays, uneven sheen on inspected surfaces
  • Every surface on the recorded walkthrough, which is the proof of scope
  • The repair itself: prep, paint and cleanup, at no cost to you

What it honestly does not

  • Structural movement, settling cracks, or substrate failure under the paint
  • Storm, impact or water damage from a source we did not create
  • Normal fading from sun and salt, which time wins eventually
  • Surfaces we flagged at the walkthrough and you chose to defer
