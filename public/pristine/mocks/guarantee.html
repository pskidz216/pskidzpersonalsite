The guarantee

We stand behind our work.

Every project includes clear written warranty information. If a legitimate workmanship issue arises within the applicable coverage, we will evaluate it and make covered corrections in accordance with the written warranty terms.

What workmanship coverage is designed to address

  • Covered adhesion or application failures attributable to our workmanship
  • Covered finish defects attributable to our application
  • The painted surfaces and scope identified in your written estimate
  • Covered corrective work performed according to the written warranty terms

What it does not generally cover

  • Structural movement, settling, substrate failure or conditions beneath the coating
  • Storm, impact, leaks or water intrusion from sources outside our work
  • Normal weathering, fading and maintenance associated with coastal exposure
  • Existing conditions, exclusions or deferred repairs identified in the project scope

If something does not look right

01

Call or text us and describe what you are seeing. Photos are helpful when available.

02

We review the concern against the written project scope, products and applicable warranty terms and determine the appropriate next step.

03

If the issue is covered by our workmanship warranty, we address it according to the written warranty terms.

Clear coverage, in writing.

Your estimate identifies the products and coating system for your project, and the applicable warranty coverage is provided in writing so you know what is covered.
