The guarantee

We stand behind our work.

We take responsibility for our work. Warranty coverage varies based on the products and coating system selected for your project, and your specific coverage is clearly outlined in your estimate — not in fine print you find later.

What we stand behind

  • Our workmanship: preparation and application, backed by Pristine Coastal
  • Finish defects such as lap marks, holidays or uneven sheen
  • Product and coating-system coverage as specified in your estimate
  • The repair itself: prep, paint and cleanup on covered work

What it honestly does not

  • Structural movement, settling cracks, or substrate failure under the paint
  • Storm, impact or water damage from a source we did not create
  • Normal fading from sun and salt, which time wins eventually
  • Surfaces we flagged at the walkthrough and you chose to defer
