Pristine Coastal Co. concepts, August 2026

Start with the map, then pick a motion.

The site map shows how the whole site is put together. Then three homepages. All three share the Deep Harbor palette below, the same copy and the same photographs, so the only variable between them is the motion. A is static. B is parallax: a walk from the path, through the front door, into the room. C runs the scroll-choreography recipe, which controls time rather than simulating space, and uses no parallax at all.

Concept A

Static

The baseline: no motion system at all, the page simply is. Coral is 2.63:1 on shell, so it cannot carry text; every button and link is the harbor blue, and coral appears twice on the whole page.

Concept B

Outside in

The same Deep Harbor palette as A. What changes is the hero: a parallax push from the crushed shell path, through the front door, into the room, and down onto the paint itself.

Concept C

Choreographed

Same palette, no parallax anywhere: the choreography conducts time instead of space. The hero cycles three finished homes across one day, six beats a frame, from a morning street in the historic district to an oceanfront noon to a beach house at dusk, while the headline arrives on the downbeat. Sections land on a 700ms beat, lists enumerate at 60ms, and the four step process plays as a pinned slideshow, each step with its own photograph.

What is real

The four step process, the veteran and pilot line, the ten service names, military discounts and the boutique positioning all come from the live site. The palettes and type come from the approved brand sheet.

What is placeholder

Every price and rate. The live site publishes no rates, no license number, no review count and no warranty, so neither do these. All fifteen photographs are generated, for art direction only.

Two things to settle

The live site says 22 years in its FAQ and 23 elsewhere, so these pages use 22 throughout. And the name: these carry Pristine Coastal Co., which is not yet signed off.
