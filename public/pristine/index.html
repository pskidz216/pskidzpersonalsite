Start with the map, then walk the build.

The site map shows how the whole site is put together. Then the build itself: two homepage directions on the same architecture, brand and photography, plus the two inner pages that do the search work. V1 is the editorial direction with the full motion system, smooth scroll, parallax, a horizontal neighborhood gallery and a drawer menu. V2 is the calmer contractor direction with light parallax and conventional dropdowns. Real project photographs from the live site are in both.

Neighborhood & Pricing

The two page types that win search. Ponte Vedra Beach as the neighborhood archetype: an answer-first opening, coordinates on the photograph, real oceanfront work and the questions people actually ask. The pricing page, with the calculator and published ranges, is one click away in the menu above.

What is real

The architecture, the palette and type from the brand bible, and the project photographs on the work surfaces, which come from the live site. The veteran line, ten service names and boutique positioning all come from the business.

What is placeholder

Every price and range, marked as such on the pricing page until the real numbers land. Scenic photographs, the neighborhood cards and the hero, are generated for art direction.

What was retired

The three motion concepts from the July round. Their decision is folded into these two builds: V1 carries the choreography and parallax, V2 carries the restraint.
