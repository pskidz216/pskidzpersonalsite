What is real

The four step process, the veteran and pilot line, the ten service names, military discounts and the boutique positioning all come from the live site. The palettes and type come from the approved brand sheet.

What is placeholder

Every price and rate. The live site publishes no rates, no license number, no review count and no warranty, so neither do these. All fifteen photographs are generated, for art direction only.

Two things to settle

The live site says 22 years in its FAQ and 23 elsewhere, so these pages use 22 throughout. And the name: these carry Pristine Coastal Co., which is not yet signed off.
